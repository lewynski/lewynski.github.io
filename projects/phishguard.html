System Architecture

Dual-Layer Detection

PhishGuard combines client-side phishing heuristics with VirusTotal vendor verdicts for a broader URL reputation check.

Privacy First

All local analysis happens in your browser. Optional VirusTotal checks are made directly from your device with your control. History stored locally only.

Secure Backend

Your VirusTotal API key is stored securely on Vercel servers. Only threat analysis requests are sent to external APIs. Your data is never logged or retained.
